Hi, I'm Victor Onwunle

I'm Onwunle Victor, a technology entrepreneur and product builder from Nigeria, the founder of SchoolBox, an education-focused technology project, and the mind behind several other concepts and products currently in development. My interests extend across software development, product architecture, cybersecurity, and emerging technologies.

My journey into technology began with cybersecurity. That early exposure shaped how I think — analytical, detail-oriented, and always looking beneath the surface and thinking beyond what is immediately visible. That curiosity gradually expanded into software development and product creation.

I am particularly interested in building practical technology that addresses real-world problems. I believe great products require more than technical ability; they demand strategic thinking, creativity, an understanding of people, and the discipline to turn ideas into functional solutions. Beyond technology, I appreciate different cultures and perspectives, and believe that meaningful business relationships are built on trust, collaboration, and shared vision. I am continuously learning, experimenting, and developing new ideas. My ambition is to contribute to the global technology ecosystem by building products that are useful today and relevant to the possibilities of tomorrow. I am open to meaningful opportunities, international collaborations, remote positions, and conversations with people building interesting things.

objective: understand technology, build useful things, and create solutions that matter
